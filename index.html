ただいま MJ06 RESEARCH

Research directions

SERVICE DESIGN

Adaptive Design for Aging

Most furniture is built around standard sizes, while older adults' bodies and support needs vary widely. This concept uses AI agents and 3D printing to generate furniture fitted to one person, their measurements and their room, previewed in AR at real scale before fabrication.

AI agentsXR3D printingPersonalized furniture
INCLUSIVE DESIGN

Pressure Cocoon

How might a breathing-responsive environment support emotional regulation through bodily interaction?

Deep pressure can reduce anxiety and support sensory regulation, but weighted blankets and squeeze machines are passive and can feel restrictive. Pressure Cocoon is a soft, semi-open inflatable space that follows the user's breathing, so the user stays in control of the pressure they receive.
